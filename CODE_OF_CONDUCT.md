# Code of Conduct

Aidline exists to help people in hard moments. We want working on it to feel the same way: welcoming, patient and respectful.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Many contributors are new to open source or to Stellar.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Explain the why behind a review comment.
- Respect different backgrounds, languages and time zones. Our users and contributors come from all over the world.
- Ask before you assume. A question in an issue is always welcome.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Discriminatory language or jokes
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Spamming issues or pull requests, including low effort submissions made only to claim rewards
- Pressuring maintainers or other contributors for reviews, merges or payouts

## Scope

This applies to every Aidline space: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and any channel where you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone makes you feel unsafe or unwelcome, contact the maintainers privately by messaging [@sweetsoul21](https://github.com/sweetsoul21) on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, close pull requests, or block accounts that break these rules. For serious or repeated problems, a contributor may be banned from the project.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
